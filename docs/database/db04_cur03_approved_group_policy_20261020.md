## 1. Approved product behavior

| Decision | Approved behavior | Deliberate exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| **CUR-03-A1 — one group per elective rule** | Every **ELECTIVE** Subject Rule belongs to **exactly one** published choice group within its immutable Curriculum revision. **OPTIONAL** remains independently selectable and does not occupy an ELECTIVE group. **COMPULSORY** is derived from the published Rule and never joins a choice group or generates a Student selection row. Subject can belong to a different group in a *different* revision without reusing history. | Same-revision multi-group, optional-as-group substitute, ungrouped ELECTIVE. |
| **CUR-03-B1 — configured group bounds and atomic finalization** | A school configures **nonnegative integer** `min_choices` and `max_choices` per choice group in each published revision. No global minimum/maximum number is imposed. An **accepted finalized elective set** is atomic and must satisfy every applicable group's bounds, including groups with zero selected Subjects. Finalization may submit multiple valid choices in one operation; no partial accepted batch violates a positive minimum. D1 PRIMARY Enrollment remains valid before elective finalization. | No automatic elective selection, enrollment rollback, partial accepted set bypass or inferred group completion from a Student's presence in School. |
| **CUR-03-C1 — prerequisites deferred** | V1 does **not** enforce prerequisite Subjects, co-requisites, previous-year credits or Exam-pass thresholds; reject any attempted use of unsupported prerequisite declarations rather than ignore them or invent a mark threshold. | No prerequisite DAG/table or Exams attestation interface approved. |
| **CUR-03-D1 — no seat allocation** | V1 Curriculum election has **no** seat quotas, waitlists, lotteries, rank-based allocation or seat-reservation engine. Section Subject Offering eligibility is not a guarantee of physical classroom/Timetable capacity. | No seat counter/hold/allocation table; no inferred teacher/room capacity. |
| **CUR-03-E1 — no automatic deadline/exception** | V1 imposes **no generic selection deadline**, late-change penalty, automatic approval, exceptional override or academic-year automatic elective assignment. Changes still require exact authorized action, applicable dates and historical integrity under separately approved CUR-04/05/06. | No globally preconfigured cut-off or silent grandfathering rule. |

**Group bound scope:** Validate per exact D1 School/Class Offering/Section Offering/Academic Year and effective-date interval; no globally unique ACTIVE year. Count **distinct currently valid elected Subject IDs** tied to the exact accepted PRIMARY Enrollment, published Revision and Group, rather than arbitrary matching display names. A selected Subject must be effectively offered to that exact Section, in the same immutable Revision and Group, and currently accepted with a valid Student selection history.

**Original B1 wording:** the approved candidate B1 proposal explicitly states `0 ≤ min ≤ max ≤ distinct effective offered group members` for every affected Section/date segment. [Independent review F-02](db04_cur03_group_interval_lock_and_decision_status_review_20261020.md) later offered B-M2, treating `max` only as a cap and requiring just `min ≤ offered members`. **No B-M2 choice was expressly given; do not relax the original B1 rule under this approval.** Preserve strict B1 for design/negative tests until the owner explicitly approves a change, and obtain a final B-M signoff before irreversible exclusion/trigger implementation because the competing interpretation was raised in owner review. The ambiguous case `min=1,max=2,available=1` is the discriminating fixture.

> **Verified Foundation receipt follow-up (2026-10-20):** [B-F receipt capability and schema options audit](db04_cur03_finalization_receipt_capability_and_schema_options_20261020.md) confirms `command_receipts` has idempotency/typed result fields but **no indexed Student+Enrollment+revision finalization head**. ## 2. Physical/integration questions *not* settled by owner approval

**B-F finalized-empty evidence:** With `min=0` a valid final batch can contain **zero selected Subjects**. Candidate `student_subject_selections` stores elected Subjects only; zero rows alone cannot distinguish **no submission** from **submitted/finalized with zero**. The B1 product choice **approves atomic finalization**, but **not a particular implementation of finalization evidence**. Before CUR-08 physical freeze, review whether an already-approved Foundation command receipt can provide immutable, re-queryable, appropriately scoped Student+Enrollment+revision finalization evidence, including a subsequent changed set, or whether a dedicated DB-04 finalization fact is necessary. **Do not invent a fake Subject choice, mutate D1 Enrollment, claim seven tables suffice, or treat a receipt as sufficient without verifying its installed schema/query and history.**

**CUR-08 schema count:** Seven DB-04 relations remain a *candidate*. Subject Rules' typed `category`, `choice_group_id` and one-to-one group membership fit A1. `curriculum_choice_groups` supplies revision-scoped bounded integers; `student_subject_selections` supplies immutable accepted electives. However B-F, independent Practical/Custom classification fields approved conceptually under CUR-02, and robust bitemporal correction/constraint enforcement may change columns or entity count. No column/FK/extension/triggers granted by this product approval.

**CUR-01 timing and CUR-06 revisions:** The owner-approved nonoverlap policy prohibits school-wide and Campus-specific conflicting curricula for one Class Offering/date. **Future-effective or mid-year replacements** and their effect on existing choices are not automatically authorized by CUR-03. Publication must not orphan accepted/current/future selections; old revision/group/selection remains reconstructable, and a Student's D1 Enrollment MOVE creates a different source Enrollment rather than reparenting elections.

**CUR-04/05 authorization/workflow:** The approving School Owner has **not** authorized Student, FAMILY or assigned teachers to mutate choices. Verified Principal, exact grant and scope, distinct reviewer, configured P0/P1/P2 action policy, typed receipt/audit/outbox and live checks remain separate decisions and Foundation/deployment-owned registration. School-first D1/DB-04 lock compatibility is only source-derived until independently proven with real two-session runtime evidence.

**CUR-07 downstream snapshots:** Exams/Learning/Timetable must explicitly accept immutable revision/group/Rule/Section offering/accepted Student selection IDs with `valid_at` and `observed_at` and their own authorized snapshot freeze points. No downstream module source or schema is modified in DB-04.

## 3. Acceptance obligations (proposed, NOT executed)

1. Published group must have one and only one membership per ELECTIVE Rule per Revision, no OPTIONAL/COMPULSORY cross-count; repeated group code in newer Revision must not reuse prior Group ID or Student election.
2. `min≥0`, `max≥min`, and—under the original B1 constraint until revised—`max≤distinct actually offered group members` for **every relevant Section/date segment**. If strict max option changes, test `min=1,max=2,available=1` to distinguish B-M1/B-M2; do not silently change the expected result.
3. One authorized *finalized atomic batch* can select several different Subject IDs satisfying bounds; same Subject twice never satisfies a minimum twice. A Student with no accepted final batch still has valid D1 Enrollment and no individual elective Exam eligibility.
4. Future-effective withdrawal/revision/Section Subject Offering closure is checked at **each interval breakpoint**; no stranded accepted selected head and no partial success.
5. Two concurrent group selections/amendments on the **same Student+Enrollment+Revision** serialize under the reviewed School→Student→Enrollment→D1 academic→Curriculum fence. Opposing D1 MOVE/END and Subject ARCHIVE paths must be verified against the full assembled D1 and all FK/deferred locks, not assumed deadlock-free.
6. Attempted prerequisite, capacity, selection deadline, or generic automatic exception behavior is rejected as unsupported; no silent bypass or new engine.
7. B-F no-Subject finalized-empty vs not-submitted requires **separate acceptance evidence only after an explicit physical implementation decision**.
8. Historical correction must preserve original Exam's as-observed Curriculum/selection snapshot regardless of later corrections.
9. Wrong School/Campus/Academic Year/Section/Enrollment and unauthorized teacher/FAMILY actions deny without leaking private records.
10. No test is marked passing, and no migration/catalog registration is implied.
