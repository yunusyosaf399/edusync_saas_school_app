# DB08 F3 — approved future fee price change candidate

F3 adds a protected request and independent review path for a later price on one published School/Campus/AcademicYear rule. It retains an immutable typed intent, a Foundation approval request/step/reviewer/decision, predecessor-linked price revision and publication, and separate receipt/audit/outbox facts for each command. Only the final approval publishes the successor. Rejection retains the proposal without a price revision. All changes are in an inactive draft and must pass disposable acceptance before any activation.

The School configures an active Foundation approval policy for the `finance.fee.change` operation with one `DIRECT_ROLE` reviewer template and `ALL/DIRECT` request and review permissions. A requester nominates a reviewer who currently holds that configured role and review permission. The reviewer must be a different, active Auth-linked individual. Current authorization, configured policy, reviewer assignment and target price are rechecked after the ordered lock. Two or more review steps, an absent or inactive policy, unknown resolver, or unqualified reviewer deny without publishing. This slice does not establish a school-wide default review chain or approval threshold.

The requested price is an exact integer minor-unit amount. The effective date must be in the future and later than the prior revision start, within the Academic Year; no preexisting obligation may end on or after the proposed start. Review checks these conditions again and rejects a stale predecessor. An approved revision retains its predecessor and starts a new price period. Assessment of a period before that start can still use the old published revision; a period at or crossing the new start must use the newer revision within its validity. Existing obligations are never repriced. Further adjustments, refunds, multi-step approvals, recurring class/Student selectors, discounts, payment and Finance clearance remain separate tasks.

The F3 test is rollback-only and uses synthetic local Auth-linked staff and an explicit synthetic policy. The disposable CI installs F1/F2, rehearses failed F3 installation and rollback, installs F3, checks pending/approval/rejection/replay/current-price behavior, then repeats F2 real Auth and three observed-backend contention cases on the combined schema. No hosted School database is modified. The exact CI outcome is recorded in the draft PR.

Example: tuition is 100.00 for April. An accountant requests 120.00 from November 1; the price stays 100.00 while pending. A different authorized reviewer approves. April keeps 100.00 and a new November assessment uses 120.00. Rejecting a later request produces no new price.
