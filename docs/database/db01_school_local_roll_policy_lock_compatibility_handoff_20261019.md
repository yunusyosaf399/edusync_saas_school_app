## 3. Verified lock graph and missing MOVE source namespace

**History trigger:** final `effect30_33_qualification_experience_lineage_guard.sql.draft::d1_guard_effective_history()`, on Roll INSERT/UPDATE, locks **School→the stored source policy→mode-specific source allocator→Student**. `effect30_32::d1_guard_crossrow` also locks academic path and checks history. No public command may acquire Student and only later the School/source allocator.

**END:** #106's `effect37_02_db01_end_source_prelock.sql.draft::d1_enrollment_end_lock_context()` first discovers the Enrollment's source Section and Roll policy, locks School, sorted stored policies and sorted matching allocator rows, then locks Student and original Enrollment/roll context. This fixes the recognized source-lock inversion in source order for END. Verify the exact final assembled wrapper and trigger lock graph in local PostgreSQL with genuine two-session block; sequential suites #76 (30/30) are not a concurrency proof.

**MOVE:** final `effect26_05_enrollment_move_direct_submit.sql.draft` and `effect26_09_enrollment_move_apply_hardening.sql.draft` prelock only the **destination** effective policy/allocator via `d1_enrollment_place_prelock_roll(destination_section,effective_date)` before Student, then source context/effect updates historic source Roll. A *different source* revision, or the academic source Offering, may require an as-yet-unheld allocator lock within the BEFORE UPDATE trigger **after** Student. Destination-only prelock is not enough.

### Exact D1 integration-owned MOVE patch acceptance specification (do NOT edit here)

1. For DIRECT, SUBMIT and APPLY, preflight source Student/Enrollment/Section/roll and destination Offering with read-only discovery, **without trusting stale findings**. Identify actual source-policy and source-pool ID, destination policy effective at proposed date (T-B-consistent when current-day), and destination-pool ID. Require pre-existing allocator rows as current physical contract demands.
2. Lock **one School** (reject cross-School or stale ancestry) first. Re-read source/target School and relevant policies after waiting. Lock the **union of source and destination policy UUIDs sorted globally** (not destination then an earlier source policy), then the **union of allocator IDs sorted globally** before Student. When both pools are same, lock once. Include historical source policy even if **expired today**: MOVE/END closing old Roll is legal without pretending old policy is today's policy. Revalidate all collected IDs after locks, and on drift deny/restart under reviewed Foundation idempotency rules.
3. Only after the complete prefix lock Student (UUID ascending when multiple), academic hierarchy, Enrollment/roll and capacity. Preserve existing MOVE effects, route, scope, capacity override, immutable origin/roll carry and receipt/event replay logic. The Roll trigger must request **only locks already pre-acquired**, never a different School or later additional allocator.
4. Genuine PostgreSQL observed-wait tests: CORRECT vs MOVE; MOVE r1 source→r2 destination under policy replacement; MOVE vs END; MOVE vs P0 allocator issue; both winner orders; a rollback and a lost-response retry. Observer uses `pg_backend_pid()`, `pg_stat_activity.wait_event_type='Lock'`, `pg_blocking_pids(waiter_pid)` and `pg_locks`; no sleep-only fake contention or empty shared fixtures. Ensure source Rn's historical effective_until is preserved, new Enrollment Roll origin references current persistent usable head, and exactly one receipt/outbox per committed effect.
