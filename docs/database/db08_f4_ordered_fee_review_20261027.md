# DB08 F4 — ordered fee price review candidate

F4 extends the F3 approved price-change path to configured Foundation approval policies with two through eight ordered `DIRECT_ROLE` reviewer steps. The requester supplies one distinct current eligible reviewer for each configured step. The existing one-reviewer RPC remains available for a one-step policy; it rejects a longer policy. Reviewers must be distinct from the requester and from each other, hold the configured role and live `finance.fee.change.review` permission with School-wide `ALL/DIRECT` scope, and remain current when they act.

At submission, every step and assignment is retained, but only step one opens. An approval closes the current step and opens the next, retaining the request in `PENDING` and issuing an immutable command receipt/audit/outbox event. Only the final approval appends the price revision and publication, then marks the request `EXECUTED`. A rejection at any step closes that step, cancels all future waiting steps and retains the rejected request without a new price. Every review checks its expected request version and exact idempotency key. After a backend waits on the ordered authorization lock, it checks current actor, policy, chain and fee source again.

Policies without contiguous steps starting at one, policies with more than eight steps, unsupported reviewer resolver or review count, duplicate or unqualified reviewers, and out-of-order decisions fail closed. The eight-step limit is a bounded initial workflow implementation, not a School commercial threshold. No default policy, reviewer, fee threshold, discount or clearance is invented. Existing obligations remain at their assessed amounts.

Disposable acceptance installs F1–F3, rehearses failed F4 DDL and rollback, then exercises the two-step request, intermediate approval, exact replay, final publication and second-step rejection with transaction-scoped Auth-linked fixtures. F2/F3 real Auth tests rerun on the combined schema. Exact run evidence belongs in the draft PR after observation; nothing is activated in a hosted School project.

Example: a School configures two independent reviewers. The first approves a proposed November tuition price of 120.00; the price remains 100.00. The second approves, and only then do new November assessments use 120.00. If the second rejects, the price stays 100.00.
