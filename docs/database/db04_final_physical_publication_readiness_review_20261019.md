## A. Seven-entity independent physical decision

**Decision recommendation: RETAIN ALL SEVEN as the smallest practical baseline for section-specific subject availability and durable Student choices.** They are distinct from D1 and none should be silently removed. The earlier alternative of six tables would be justified only if the product owner explicitly rejects section-local availability, closed/limited section subjects and independently referenceable section offering IDs. Do **not** make a “six instead of seven” migration from that unapproved assumption.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | §curriculum_definitions§ | One school-configurable named curriculum; not a D1 Class or Subject | §school_id→school_profiles§; unique (school_id,code); immutable code/school; audited ACTIVE→ARCHIVED with row_version | Retain |

A separate Curriculum publication/application ledger is not automatically necessary if existing **Foundation receipts/workflows** plus revision/applicability business timestamps prove history; however the schema needs an *accepted_at* and *end-recorded-at* knowledge-time proof. If those cannot be expressed defensibly on rows and Foundation evidence, a separately reviewed append-only transition ledger may be needed, raising the candidate count. Downstream consumer-owned immutable snapshots are NOT a duplicate DB-04 table.

**Critical schema refinements pending physical freeze:**
- The draft model currently has §curriculum_revisions.status§ and §curriculum_applicabilities.state§ but no complete evidence linking *per-applicability* replacement and “known at” time. Freeze server-owned §accepted_at§, §closed_at§, §closure_receipt_id§ or equivalent recorded-time data; §effective_until§ is exclusively business-valid time and a one-way bound. Version status §SUPERSEDED§ must not falsely claim globally superseded when some campuses/years still use that revision.
- Freeze immutable post-PUBLISH §revision_content_digest§ (canonical rule/group/content hash), publication workflow/receipt references, exactly scoped affected applicability IDs, predecessor and review evidence; no published-child UPDATE/DELETE except tightly typed one-way end of effective applicability. Revision header state may advance with audit; rule content cannot.
- Distinguish time a selection was **accepted** (§recorded_at§ / receipt commit) from the date it **applies** (§selected_from§). A retrospective correction must preserve the superseded decision as it was understood before correction. §supersedes_id§ stays same Student/enrollment/Subject lineage only; transferring Student/Section/Year is not a correction.
- Revision applicability uses explicit year+Class+optional campus because Foundation permits overlapping academic-year intervals. The authoritative Class Offering FK determines **which** academic year applies; never infer “current year” solely from wall clock. Use exact D1 PK or existing composite key; *do not invent* §campuses(id,school_id)§ or another unreviewed FK. Validate ancestry with locked server predicates.
- Either model §curriculum_section_subject_offerings§ immutable as a published interval fact plus one-way close or create a reviewed successor; mutable §availability_state§ without history is inadequate for downstream replay.
- Choice minimum is a set constraint across **all** groups, including groups with zero selection rows. The seven entities support an **atomic initial batch** tied to one Foundation command receipt. Do not accept partial effective choices violating minimum and hope a later row repairs them. Provisional Student/FAMILY requests, if approved, live in Foundation workflow, not an unprotected partial active-election table.
- §curriculum_choice_groups§/rules/offerings must be protected from contradictory duplicate/null group key races. Indexes require composite FK review and serialized parent validation; §CHECK§ cannot verify a cross-table group count.
- A row's one-way §selected_until§ after a later D1 Enrollment END is **not** mandatory if the authoritative live effective Student eligibility is always intersected with Enrollment's §[effective_from,effective_until)§; old selection stays as evidence and never follows new enrollment. Owner must choose between this clamp-only model and an explicit integration event/closure contract before SQL (CUR-06).

**Proposed indexes** (final names and INCLUDE/partial predicates require query review): definition (school_id,code); revision (curriculum_id,revision_no), (curriculum_id,status); applicability (academic_year_id,academic_class_id,campus_id,effective_from,effective_until), (revision_id,effective_from); rule (revision_id,subject_id), (revision_id,choice_group_id); group (revision_id,code); section offering (section_offering_id,effective_from,effective_until), (curriculum_applicability_id,curriculum_subject_rule_id), and derived exact Section+Subject overlap defense; selection (student_id,enrollment_id,selected_from), (enrollment_id,section_subject_offering_id,selected_from), (student_id,enrollment_id,choice_group_id,selected_from), (supersedes_id), (command_receipt_id). ## B. Consistent publication, replacement and effective-time model

**Recommendation:** a revision is **content-immutable from its first successful publish**, independently of its effective interval. A DRAFT may be edited with optimistic §row_version§ and must be VALIDATED again after any edit. VALIDATED digest includes applicability coverage, subject/group rules and choice cardinality. PUBLISH requires **separate P2 Foundation request/review/explicit apply** (proposed, not approved); validation alone never creates an effective rule.

| Typed lifecycle command | Status / durable consequence | Date/version and approval recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| CREATE_DEFINITION | New school-owned ACTIVE definition; no automatic revision | P0 / ALL-only for school-wide identity; code uniqueness and receipt |
| CREATE_REVISION | DRAFT N with predecessor bound to same definition | P0; definition lock serializes increasing N |
| DRAFT_EDIT | modifies only unpublished draft facts; resets validation state | P0; expected revision row_version; no arbitrary patch |
| VALIDATE_REVISION | VALIDATED digest and exact projected school/year/class/campus/Section coverage | P0; row_version + canonical digest; reject ambiguity, inactive new-use Subject or impossible group |
| PUBLISH_REVISION | PUBLISHED immutable content; separate dated applicability/Section offerings | **P2** proposed; approved typed Foundation request, apply re-check, §effective_from§ cannot silently be earlier than approval |
| FUTURE_PUBLISH | Same PUBLISHED content with explicit future §effective_from§ | **P2** proposed; no eligibility until that date; future overlap rechecked |
| REPLACE_PUBLISHED | New PUBLISHED N+1; predecessor's affected applicability end-bounded at cutover, not rule rewritten | **P2**; old §[start,cutover)§ and new §[cutover,end)§; existing choices preserved, transition impact approved separately |
| RETIRE | one-way end remaining applicability; keep immutable revision/content and existing consumer snapshots | **P2** proposed; disallow orphaned current/future usage unless reviewed transition |
| ARCHIVE_DEFINITION | ACTIVE→ARCHIVED only after compatible retire; retained history | **P2** proposed; future creation forbidden, history readable |
| ABANDON_DRAFT | DRAFT/VALIDATED→ABANDONED | P0 candidate; no published effect |

**Important correction to earlier conceptual state machine:** Do **not** globally flip a revision to §SUPERSEDED§ merely because one campus was replaced. It remains historically PUBLISHED, with §SUPERSEDED§ appropriate only after *all* relevant live/future applicability ended; alternatively omit that global state and derive from scoped applicability. Exact status taxonomy is CUR-01 owner decision.

**Effective interval:** School local §date§ semantics; half-open §[from,until)§. Foundation academic year has stored inclusive §ends_on§: curriculum upper bound is **the following calendar date**; never assume every school uses a globally unique ACTIVE academic year. Historical cutover must be reproducible with business time *and* acceptance/observation time. Default first publish at explicit academic-year start, future publish allowed. Mid-year cutover: P2 change plan listing affected scopes and existing selections; **fail closed by default** if existing Student selections would become ineligible and no reviewed transition plan exists. Publication must not close or re-parent Student selections. Published Exams are never reinterpreted or changed by publication.

## C. Exact Student eligibility and selection semantics

An effective Student/Subject eligibility at date §d§ exists **iff all** are true:
1. Student identity current for the authorized caller; acceptable D1 Student status per approved policy.
2. Exactly one accepted D1 PRIMARY Enrollment §E§ for this Student with §E.effective_from≤d<E.effective_until§ (NULL upper infinity), and it references the **same** Section Offering target. Do not use an imaginary §enrollments.row_version§; lock Student, compare §E.id,student_id,section_offering_id,placement_state,effective_from,effective_until,predecessor_id§ with expected snapshot, and check Student §row_version§ separately.
3. D1 Section→Class Offering→Class+Campus+Academic Year ancestry matches school and target; offerings must be eligible for **new writes** under D1 state rules. An old closed/archived offering remains a readable historical anchor, not current new-use authority.
4. Exactly one published revision/applicability matches that specific Class Offering and §d§; no school-wide/campus-specific implicit priority and no overlapping competing effective rows. Same revision required for rule, group and Section offering.
5. The D1 Subject is a valid same-school reference and usable for *new* application under archived-Subject policy; exact eligible Section-Subject offering interval contains §d§.
6. **COMPULSORY:** derives obligation from rule and D1 Enrollment without materializing a fake “elective election” record. **OPTIONAL:** eligible only if actively accepted explicitly when chosen; individual optional may be absent. **ELECTIVE:** explicit accepted selection in same Enrollment/Section subject offering, group min/max satisfied by current accepted **distinct** subject set for every touched effective segment.
7. No contradictory predecessor/history, choice group, prerequisites or unauthorized exceptions.

**Group policy recommendation:** A given revision's Subject belongs to at most one group; min/max nonnegative and max within distinct offered members. A finalized batch must satisfy *all min* and *all max*, including empty groups. An amendment/withdraw cannot violate min at any future or current time; no duplicate/equivalent overlapping elected Subject. **Multiple subjects in the same group are allowed up to max**, e.g. 2 out of 3. If user wants prerequisites, block prerequisite-bearing rules until CUR-03 approves an explicit DAG and defines whether “prerequisite” means concurrent choice, previously completed subject or Exam pass. This may require the eighth entity and Exams dependency; no text-based substitute.

**Cross-Section/Campus MOVE and new Academic Year:** existing selection facts stay tied to old Enrollment (including a future-dated D1 MOVE); new accepted Section needs separate verified election/approval. The effective read is **intersection with authoritative D1 Enrollment interval**, so an old “open” row cannot leak eligibility after MOVE/END. If a pending future selection would become impossible after MOVE, D1 remains source of truth; DB-04 rejects new application and exposes a safe stale/needs-reselection status. Silent auto-migration, grade equivalence, or roll rewrite is forbidden. Existing archived Subject may remain retained evidence, but a new ADD/re-publication cannot assume archive is an exception.

**Who may apply:** recommended **staff-only** mutation under a live complete permission and exact affected scope, P1 DIRECT/APPROVAL for current/future; P2 for historical CORRECT and any exception. Student and FAMILY are **read-only baseline**. If CUR-04 approves Student/FAMILY proposals, register a *distinct typed submit* permission/family-safe policy and exact SELF or current FAMILY CHILD scope; resulting proposal is NOT an accepted selection until separately reviewed and applied by authorized staff. Relationship, teaching assignment, Principal kind or role label alone confers no write permission.

**History:** typed INITIAL accepts an atomic ordered set of subjects; CHANGE closes specified accepted rows and inserts new choices in same accepted Enrollment; WITHDRAW one-way ends row at valid bound; CORRECT is P2 append successor with §supersedes_id§ (same Student/enrollment/lineage) and protected correction reason. Always preserve predecessor and original §recorded_at§/receipt; no backdated in-place rewrite or “reason-only” correction. If correction would conflict with already published downstream facts, stop and require CUR-07 owner-approved reconciliation—not silent retrospective recalculation.

## F. Immutable downstream eligibility and stable contracts

**Shared typed snapshot candidates**, not installed functions: §schema_version,as_of_valid_date,observed_at,school_id,academic_year_id,class_id,campus_id,class_offering_id,section_offering_id,curriculum_definition_id,revision_id,revision_no,revision_content_digest,applicability_id,subject_rule_id,section_subject_offering_id,subject_id,category,choice_group_id?,student_id?,enrollment_id?,selection_id?,selection_recorded_at?,selection_effective_from?,selection_effective_until?,eligibility_result,safe_denial_code§. - **Exams:** fixed private Exams-owned caller, single assessment-valid date/Student/Enrollment/Subject, immutable revision/rule/selection basis frozen at the Exam assessment/registration lock chosen by Exams. Exam Controller/marks publication remains exclusively Exams-owned. - **Learning:** read-only fixed section+subject descriptor and authorized per-Student recipient check, retain subject offering/revision and recipient snapshot at assignment publication, and recheck submission eligibility as Learning contract decides. - **Timetable:** section+subject/revision/availability valid-date snapshot; no Student list or elective consent claimed. Timetable separately owns scheduling/staff/room overlaps, required periods and publication.

Three **distinct fixed internal** resolver endpoints (Exams Student subject, Learning assignment/recipient, Timetable Section subject) are preferred over a client-controlled §purpose§ dispatch. SQL signatures, private EXECUTE principals, historical corrections, snapshot lifecycle and published-event compatibility require **Exams, Learning and Timetable signoff** (CUR-07).
