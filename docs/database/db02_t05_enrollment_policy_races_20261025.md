# DB-02 T05 — REPLACE versus protected Enrollment, disposable local

The T05 runner uses four independent resets of the disposable Supabase stack. Each reset applies the unchanged frozen Foundation migrations and D1 `.sql.draft` chain, provisions local Auth fixtures, and creates the one singleton School, a real source Roll Policy and allocator, a genuine earlier protected P0 Enrollment with retained Roll, two separate Students, and a destination Class Offering. The test enables only local synthetic DIRECT authority for the policy and Enrollment commands. The protected P0 seed and every raced PLACE/MOVE/END use the authenticated public commands, not direct writes to Enrollment or Roll rows.

Three races let a genuine protected REPLACE commit while holding the School lock. A distinct authenticated worker issues PLACE, MOVE or END and is observed waiting on the transaction/tuple lock held by that School-lock owner via `pg_blocking_pids`. On release, PLACE and MOVE must allocate against the new policy and its new pool; END must close the retained older placement and Roll without reparenting. Exact source closure, successor/predecessor, pool existence, retained original Roll, one Enrollment receipt/event, and no allocation without an allocator are checked after each commit.

The fourth race commits a genuine future-effective PLACE first. A concurrently authenticated REPLACE is observed waiting on the same School anchor and then denied by its freshly rerun future-commitment preflight. The future Enrollment and Roll remain under the old policy, no policy successor or receipt appears, and no orphan allocator is created. The fixture uses a future date only for this conflict case; the policy command remains strictly today-only.

This proof is draft/local only. It does not activate the operation in a hosted school project, merge migrations, or substitute for independent security review. More expansive retained-number ownership scenarios remain T07 and integration/release gates remain open.
