# DB03 owner decision: draft, forwarding, Principal approval and admission fee status

Status: accepted product direction from owner, 1 November 2026; inactive DB03 implementation and final Student handoff remain open. This supersedes prior draft gate assumptions where they conflict. It does not activate a migration or grant.

## Owner instruction and workflow

The administrator starts one application and saves incomplete information in an editable DRAFT. The school may collect missing information and the required birth and school-leaving/transfer certificates during the allowed two-month document window. Until required items are present, the application stays DRAFT with a private pending/missing-items view; do not create another application just because it is pending. The admin attaches certificates as private PDF or image files. Once required information and the applicable required certificates are present, a protected Forward action becomes available. The Principal views the application and actual private documents, reviews the details and gives the sole final admission approval or returns/rejects for correction. The Principal resolves the canonical Person during review if needed. Only a successful Principal approval completes admission and triggers one atomic Student/Student ID/admission number/initial enrollment and roll handoff against that verified Person. The administrator cannot create a Student by toggling an application field. A Principal rejection/return preserves application, files and history.

The School may configure which documents are required for an intake; birth and leaving/transfer certificates follow the owner's stated requirement, with transfer applicability expressed by the published policy. Missing evidence does not become verified by a client checkbox. Forward checks the latest file links and availability under live authorization. After forwarding, any material change invalidates the reviewed snapshot and returns the item for review. The existing separately authorized document review may be performed by the Principal, who is distinct from the administrator uploader; no extra identity verification chain is requested by this owner direction.

The administrator records the admission-fee disposition as SKIPPED, MARKED_PAID or UNPAID. It is a versioned, audited administrative declaration with actor, timestamp, reason for skip/change, and a source/revision. A displayed MARKED_PAID flag is not a bank settlement, verified payment, official receipt, or Finance ledger entry. If a real receipt/payment exists, it is linked by a separately authorized Finance source; the admission declaration never creates one. UNPAID may be forwarded and approved under this revised owner instruction; any actual payable obligation remains due and visible to Finance. SKIPPED means no admission-fee collection is required for that application, without changing unrelated tuition/transport/material charges or scholarships. Changing a prior status appends history and invalidates any pending Principal review snapshot; it does not erase a real payment.

This revises the S10 deny-only Finance-clearance gate and the older SC-03 finalization requirement for the **admission fee only**. The positive finalization command must implement the revised disposition contract and a Finance reconciliation hook where a real obligation/payment exists. Until that integration is tested, S10 remains deny-only; do not replace its guard with a permissive boolean. Student creation and file relationship handoff remain disabled in the existing SQL.

## Applicant identity and duplicate handling

DRAFT may have no Person under approved P2-D01. The administrator may save it without extensive identity verification. At Principal approval, the server checks and binds/creates the canonical Person through the protected original-inspection source and enforces PR #199's school-wide unique open Person application, the existing-Student prohibition and idempotent handoff. A name alone does not identify a child. Two opaque drafts can exist before identity is established; a possible match must be resolved privately by the Principal, with a generic conflict to another actor. Do not promise absolute same-human deduplication without an identity source.

## Implementation order and minimum acceptance

1. Preserve existing DRAFT create, private file upload/links, missing-document/grace projection and forward snapshot. Add a checked completeness source for other required form fields without exposing private applicant data in broad queues.
2. Add append-only admission-fee disposition and protected admin command/read. No client direct DML; RLS forced; receipt/audit/outbox minimized.
3. Compose forward eligibility from latest required data/documents and fee disposition (SKIPPED/MARKED_PAID/UNPAID all explicit); no inferred default paid/skip. Principal access is checked at read time.
4. Add Principal approve/return/reject decision with current authority, versions, complete requirements, actual documents, current identity and a current review snapshot. The approval effect and D1 Student handoff are one transaction. Exact replay returns the same Student result; changed intent conflicts. Rollback leaves no partial Student, numbers, enrollment, file link or official fee effect.
5. Test missing birth/transfer file, PDF/image private access, admin forward, Principal-only approve, outdated review, revoked role, unpaid and skip decisions, paid declaration versus official payment distinction, duplicate Person, repeated and competing approval, Student/number/roll uniqueness and failed rollback. Disposable CI only until release review.

Example: Ahmad's administrator saves a draft on Monday without his birth certificate. The queue says missing birth certificate; Forward is unavailable. On Thursday the admin uploads a private PDF, records UNPAID, and forwards. The Principal checks the details and file, then approves. One Student and first enrollment are created; the unpaid admission fee remains outstanding for Finance. A duplicate click returns the same Student.
