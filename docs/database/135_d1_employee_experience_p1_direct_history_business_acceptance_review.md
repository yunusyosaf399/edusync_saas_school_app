## Business behavior demonstrated by suite 45 (51 assertions)

Fixture: a verified active individual staff principal creates a real Employee via protected `app.d1_create_employee`. The new Employee does not inherit authority to change experience facts. The suite explicitly enables the `employee.experience.change` permission, operation and DIRECT scope contract, then requires an independent valid role grant, matching `ALL/DIRECT` assignment scope and active DIRECT route policy. The protected `app.d1_change_employee_experience` RPC is the only mutation entrypoint used.

- **ADD**: records historical organization, role, start/end dates, summary and reason; creates a server-generated experience snapshot and stable experience-record lineage. A second call with the same exact idempotency intent reuses the original snapshot rather than duplicating it; a reused key with changed role **or reason** is rejected.
- **CORRECT**: closes the original entry under the verified actor and creates a distinct successor snapshot with the same stable experience-record lineage and exact predecessor link. Correction must change real facts; a reason-only change or a second correction of a closed source is rejected. Original successful correction replay remains stable.
- **ARCHIVE**: closes the latest active history row; it is not hard-deleted, creates no duplicate snapshot, and another independent archive of a closed row is denied. The identical completed archive request can be replayed safely.
- **Parallel factual periods, not a race test**: two independent external-experience records with overlapping worked-date intervals are inserted through authenticated DIRECT commands, have different lineage and snapshot IDs and coexist as current history rows. This tests permitted independent overlapping histories, **not** a concurrent two-session write.
- Negative paths cover missing experience change permission/grant, missing current DIRECT policy, stale Employee version, future experience start, blank organization, unsupported DELETE action, revoked assignment scope or role grant, disabled permission, disabled DIRECT scope contract, disabled operation (including prior receipt replay) and retired policy. Direct authenticated SELECT and DELETE of private retained experience rows are denied. Savepoints restore the authorization fixtures.
- Event integrity: **five** accepted direct experience effects produce exactly **five** `D1_EMPLOYEE_EXPERIENCE_CHANGE` successful command receipts, **five** verified-INDIVIDUAL `employee.experience.change` audit events, and **five** selected safe `employee.experience_changed` outbox events; no job role title disclosed in broad outbox; no APPROVAL requests generated for these DIRECT commands. Test calls `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE`, pgTAP `finish()` and `ROLLBACK`.

**Not proved here:** the separate P1 APPROVAL submit/review/apply lifecycle, a newly added two-session experience race, a broad employee teaching-permission non-escalation proof, or cross-campus authorization configurations. The frozen product contract states that external experience itself grants no teaching/authorization rights; suite 45 does not independently measure that entire authority surface.
