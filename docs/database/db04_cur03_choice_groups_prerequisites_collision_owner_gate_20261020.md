Electives must **not** create another Enrollment; D1 Subject Teacher assignments do **not** grant elective-selection authority.

- [CUR-02 A1+B1+C1 owner decision](db04_cur02_approved_subject_category_decision_20261020.md) **approved** derived COMPULSORY obligation (no per-Student choice row); accepted OPTIONAL/ELECTIVE selections; independent Practical/Extra-curricular/Custom classification; OPTIONAL individually selectable and **ELECTIVE group-governed**. This does **not** fix cardinalities or group membership details.
- The [seven-entity candidate schema](db04_seven_entity_schema_temporal_cur_decision_package_20261020.md) proposes `curriculum_choice_groups(revision_id,code,min_choices,max_choices)`, `curriculum_subject_rules(revision_id,subject_id,category,choice_group_id?)`, immutable `curriculum_section_subject_offerings` and effective-dated `student_subject_selections`. Group limits and entire seven-table schema remain **unapproved physical design** (CUR-08).
- [Reconciled School-first D1/Curriculum locking](db04_d1_curriculum_lock_fk_concurrency_gate_20261019.md) is a **proposed cross-domain source-compatible order**, not tested DB-04 SQL: Foundation authority and receipt → School → Student and exact accepted D1 Enrollment for Student command → D1 academic/Subject parents → DB-04 revision/applicability/group/selection. Publication must never lock a Student late; real two-session validation is pending.

## 2. CUR-03 owner-choice register — five separate decisions

Each option must receive an explicit owner response. The recommended baseline is intentionally narrow, reproducible and fits the **current seven-candidate-table design**, but is **not approved** merely because it appears here.

| **CUR-03-A: group membership** | **A1:** exactly **one choice group per ELECTIVE Subject Rule per revision**; an OPTIONAL is independent and **does not occupy any elective group** in v1; COMPULSORY never joins a choice group. ### Key compatibility

CUR-02-C1 is already approved: **every ELECTIVE is group-governed**. CUR-03-A1 therefore refines the model to exactly one group per elective Rule. CUR-02's approved individually selectable OPTIONAL remains independent under candidate A1. CUR-03-B1 does **not** make all optional subjects compulsory: it makes the *published elective group minimum* binding **only when a Student's accepted final election set is finalized**. Whether being “not yet finalized” has a stored status or a School notification is a separate design concern under CUR-08; **do not manufacture acceptance on behalf of a Student**.

## 3. Precise proposed eligibility and cardinality predicates (B1 + A1 only if approved)

**Source context:** Exact published `curriculum_revision_id` and `curriculum_applicability_id` resolving uniquely by D1 `class_offering_id`, `section_offering_id` and business date `d`; exact accepted `PRIMARY enrollment_id` for Student, with effective interval covering `d`. The request's School/Year/Class/Campus, category/group membership and date policy are never trusted if client-supplied.

**For each Student `S`, accepted Enrollment `E`, revision `R`, group `G` and day `d`:**

`M(R,G,section,d)` = distinct D1 Subject IDs attached to exactly one published `curriculum_subject_rule` of `R` with category ELECTIVE, group `G` and an effective, same-Revision `curriculum_section_subject_offering` for that Section and date. Do not count archived new-use Subjects as newly offered unless CUR-06 approves a narrowly defined grandfather exception. `M` counts actual Section availability, not global revision membership or teacher allocations.

`A(S,E,R,G,d)` = distinct **accepted** chosen Subject IDs for `S` in exact `E` and `R/G` with `selected_from ≤ d < effective_until`, valid D1 Enrollment interval, effective published applicability and Section-Subject offering, and no superseded/corrected accepted head at the knowledge cut-off. A correction never deletes its historical predecessor. Only count a validated canonical current-head selection once; duplicate rows cannot satisfy minimum twice. If a Student moves Section/Year, `E` changes and old selections never carry over.

**At revision/Section publication:** require `0 ≤ min(G) ≤ max(G) ≤ |M(R,G,section,d)|` for **every affected date segment** within effective publication, including days after a planned offering close/open, not just initial start. If an applicable Section has zero available members and `min(G)>0`, **deny publication** (or exclude that Section through a separately approved applicability design); do not weaken constraints silently. Other, non-group OPTIONAL offerings do not count toward ELECTIVE group bounds.

**At an accepted FINAL choice/change over proposed effective interval:** for each relevant `G` and each effective breakpoint `d`, require `min(G) ≤ |A(S,E,R,G,d)| ≤ max(G)`. Also deny a selected Subject not in `M`, duplicate overlapping Subject choices and a source Enrollment/Section/School mismatch. **Breakpoints** include the operation's interval boundaries, all accepted choice starts/ends, D1 Enrollment effective boundary, Section offering/applicability starts/ends and planned published cutover; check every resulting half-open segment, not just request date. All groups—including zero-row groups—are enumerated from the published revision; one group's satisfaction does not substitute for another's.

**Initial finalization:** a School admin can submit a **batch** of elective choices atomically, rather than accepting sequential single choices that temporarily violate a positive group min. Accepted independent OPTIONAL choices can be submitted separately if CUR-04/05 authorize them. Before finalization a Student's D1 Enrollment is unchanged; *not chosen* OPTIONAL/ELECTIVE Subjects have no accepted Student-specific Exam/Learning eligibility. **The seven-table candidate does not yet have a dedicated finalization state record.** If the owner needs a durable `FINALIZED`/`NOT_FINALIZED` audit or exact incomplete-list deadline, CUR-08 must explicitly select a persisted representation (e.g., a reviewed command receipt + versioned completion projection, or a separately approved relation); do not assume that zero rows proves a final choice was submitted.

**Amend/withdrawal:** determine the *resulting* selected set after closing old choices and adding replacements at the same effective boundary. If min would fail or max would overflow for any applicable interval, reject the whole command without partial close/add, receipt success or event. If the School revises or retires a Group/Section offering and existing future choices would be stranded, publication fails closed absent a separately reviewed transition path (CUR-01 timing, CUR-05 approval, CUR-06 grandfather/migration). Old Student selection and Exam evidence remains retained even if the current revised group changes.

**Historical correction:** a P2 route is only a CUR-05 recommendation. Preserve original acceptance/recorded-time history, append a linked successor and revalidate every affected **business-time segment** for the Student under the appropriate revision/Enrollment context. A previously published Exam's consumer-owned eligibility snapshot cannot be silently rewritten; cross-consumer correction policy is CUR-07. Do not bypass min rules with generic admin privilege.

## 4. Physical-model consequences of each CUR-03 choice

**Under candidate A1+B1+C1+D1+E1:** all **seven** existing candidate relations remain sufficient *for selection eligibility semantics*, subject to CUR-08's separate recorded-time, classification and finalization evidence review. The proposed `curriculum_choice_groups` already has per-revision `min_choices,max_choices`; `curriculum_subject_rules.choice_group_id` can remain single nullable FK with typed invariant **ELECTIVE must reference exactly one same-revision group; COMPULSORY and OPTIONAL must not**. `student_subject_selections` references exact Student+Enrollment+Section offering and derives group from frozen Rule; no new group membership table or seat allocator is proposed.

**Required indexes and locks are candidates, not SQL:** unique `(revision_id,code)` on groups; rule unique `(revision_id,subject_id)` and supporting `(revision_id,choice_group_id)`; Section offering time-range/Section-Subject indexes; Student choice `(student_id,enrollment_id,selected_from)`, selection head/`supersedes_id` and group occupancy index (either immutable verified group key or protected join). A row-local `CHECK` can enforce `0≤min≤max` but **cannot enforce `max≤distinct Section offerings`**, Student aggregate minima/maxima, graph cycles or effective-time overlap across FK joins. These require protected aggregate locking, accepted revision digest/version recheck, and possibly indexed range/exclusion protection after physical key review.

**Group cross-revision identity:** `group_id` belongs to a specific immutable `revision_id`. A Group code (e.g. `SCI`) repeated in a successor revision is **not the same group ID** and cannot silently reuse old Student choices or old Exam eligibility. Historical `Student+Enrollment+group_id+revision_id` decisions are retained; a future revised group requires its own accepted selection and change plan (CUR-06), not in-place member/limit rewriting.

**If A3/C2/C3/D2/E2 chosen:** do **not** assert seven entities still suffice. Record an explicit physical-design change request for versioned multi-membership/prerequisite/capacity/deadline evidence and role/privacy review; additional tables are likely. Exams pass and subject equivalence are **Exams and Academic owner decisions**, not DB-04 guesses.

**Never modify:** D1 Subject/Enrollment implementations; DB-01/02 Roll, DB-03 Admissions, shared permission catalog, Foundation, Exams/Learning/Timetable sources, CI registration, existing migrations/tests, or hosted Supabase. All future permission codes, P0/P1/P2 routes, denial-code strings and approval reviewers remain **candidates** until CUR-04/05 and deployment owner registration.
