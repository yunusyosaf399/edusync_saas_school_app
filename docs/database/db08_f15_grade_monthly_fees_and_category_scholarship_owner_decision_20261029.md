# DB08 F15 — grade monthly fees and applicant-specific category scholarship

**Owner clarification, 29 October 2026.** The examples of 1,000 SAR for Grade 1 and 1,200 SAR for Grade 2 are **monthly grade tuition**, not a one-time admission fee. The school configures the full fee for each grade and fee category, with School/Campus/AcademicYear context. The full fee remains the published gross price for every applicant in that grade unless a separately authorized individual discount applies.

An authorized school administrator may propose a scholarship or discount for **one named applicant** before Student creation. The decision must say which fee categories it affects. A 50% tuition-only scholarship for Grade 1 Ahmad makes his monthly tuition 500 SAR from a 1,000 SAR gross; transport, books, materials and other categories stay at their published full amounts. If the school selects transport too, Finance applies the approved 50% instruction to transport's own assessed gross charge; it does not discount an unselected category. The school may configure other categories, and a one-time item such as books must not silently become monthly merely because tuition is monthly.

## Selected behavior and retained facts

- The school selects the affected category or categories explicitly for the applicant. Every category has its own frequency, gross charge, effective period, and adjustment; one percentage is applied to each selected category's gross charge. A fixed amount, if later selected under the existing F6 choice, needs an unambiguous **per-category** amount, not an undocumented division across categories.
- The category-scoped concession identifies the application and current authorized terms revision before Student creation. An independent Finance reviewer must approve it under current scope before it affects an assessed charge. The eventual Student-bound instruction must link to the accepted Admissions decision and the resulting Student; it must not invent a Student ID while the application is still a draft.
- A percentage applies to each eligible charge separately using the existing two-decimal money rule: integer minor units, nearest minor unit, exact half upward. For a 1,000.00 tuition charge, 50% is 500.00 off and 500.00 net. A 200.00 transport charge remains 200.00 when transport was not selected, or becomes 100.00 when the same approved percentage explicitly includes transport.
- Gross fee, category selection, discount value, assessed adjustment and remaining amount are distinct retained facts. Changing an applicant's scholarship creates a new authorized revision; it does not rewrite earlier charges or prior receipts. Overlapping effective instructions for the same Student/category/charge fail closed; different categories may each have one eligible instruction.
- Discount approval never marks a charge paid. The applicant's bank slip amount is evidence of a transfer, not the published grade fee. Finance must allocate actual official payments to obligations and issue current clearance before final admission, including where a selected category reaches zero.

## Existing draft and next implementation boundary

DB08 F5 already selects published fee rules for an existing Student/placement, and F6 supports independently approved recurring `MONTHLY_TUITION` or one `SPECIFIC_CHARGE` discount. F6 does **not** yet support a recurring named non-tuition category or a pre-Student applicant source. DB03 currently has no selected grade/placement on its unresolved DRAFT application. Therefore these new choices are approved business requirements, **not a claim that the existing SQL implements them**.

The next schema increments must first retain the applicant's intended grade/destination and authorized commercial decision, then bind grade/category fee rules to the exact School/Campus/year and extend F6 eligibility to explicitly selected categories. Keep one active instruction per category and charge, independent review, source revision binding, and negative tests proving tuition-only cannot discount transport/books. Separately decide which categories are recurring versus one-time through the fee rule's frequency; do not hardcode books as monthly. One-time admission fee pricing and waivers remain a distinct Finance/Admissions question from this monthly tuition example.

**F6 category extension candidate:** the inactive Student Finance draft now provides `app.request_finance_category_discount` for a single named monthly category, with the same independent approval and per-charge adjustment path as tuition. Its category is a typed School-owned Finance category; overlapping instructions on the same Student/category/period deny, while a different category can have its own approved instruction. The candidate test checks transport's 50% adjustment and verifies an unselected one-time books charge remains full price. This is the **post-Student Finance side** of the rule. It does not yet assign a scholarship to an applicant or copy an Admissions decision into a Student after creation; DB03's missing destination/Student handoff must be solved separately before claiming that end-to-end path.
