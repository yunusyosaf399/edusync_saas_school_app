# Domain package 01 (D1A) — scope and invariants

**Status:** SELECTED conceptual boundaries, updated by [approved D1B0 product decisions](../decisions/DOMAIN_PACKAGE_01_APPROVED_DECISIONS.md). Physical design may proceed; no SQL or activation is authorized. **D1A source baseline:** `2f6f5a2632d03235c9547754126ba9ddd5c174da`.

## Authority and package boundary

The [master specification](../requirements/School_OS_SaaS_Master_Specification_v0.2.md), especially §§6–13, 16–21, 38–39, 53–58, 67–72 and the v0.2 decision history, establishes configurable academic structure, permanent student and enrollment history, shared family login, and assignment-bounded teaching. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [decisions](../decisions/DECISIONS_AND_INVARIANTS.md), [database guardrails](SAAS_AND_DATABASE_GUARDRAILS.md) and [security rules](../security/SECURITY_WORKFLOW_AND_AUDIT.md) control interpretation. The former [domain boundaries](../architecture/01_domain_boundaries.md) / [next-phase plan](DATABASE_DESIGN_NEXT_PHASE.md) subject-package ambiguity is resolved by approved D1-01: Subject catalog in D1; curriculum assignment later.

**Included:** one configurable Class/Grade identity with optional stage/category; campus/year class and section offerings; stable Subject catalog; both capacity limits and configurable roll modes; student profile, lifecycle and primary enrollment progression; multiple family/guardian relationships and separate Emergency Contact; employee profile, Department/Designation, effective campus affiliation and teaching capability/assignments; typed scope-resolver contracts. These are conceptual responsibilities, not approved SQL relation names or final field sets. Details are in [the entity map](25_domain_package_01_conceptual_entity_map.md), [dependency order](26_domain_package_01_dependency_order.md) and [resolver contracts](../security/05_domain_scope_resolver_contracts.md).

**Foundation anchors remain unchanged:** the existing 33 tables, 12 School OS roles, 49 policies, 39 application `SECURITY DEFINER` functions, `app`/unexposed `app_private` split, `people`, `principals` and Auth bindings, school profile, campuses, rooms, academic years, complete-grant evaluator, approval core, audit/outbox, command receipts and `file_objects`. D1A proposes no modification to `current_principal_id()` or `has_complete_grant(...)`. A campus is a school-internal scope; one school remains one Supabase project. Future domain entities refer to these anchors and must pass the established default-deny, protected-command and actor-attribution model.

**Excluded:** full admissions workflow, Finance/admission fee, class/year curriculum composition, optional/elective selections, assessment components and passing rules, timetable, attendance, learning, exams/results, HR salary/payroll/contracts/leave, medical, custody/court-order/pickup workflows, storage adapter, provider buckets, notification delivery, AI/search projections and Flutter. The later admissions process may carry an applicant through configurable application, verification, test, interview, approval and fee-outcome stages without creating a student prematurely. At successful handoff Admissions allocates a permanent admission number; the Student domain creates the Student and allocates its separate permanent Student ID. No direct Finance primary-key dependency is imposed. Timetable and attendance consume offerings and assignments later; they do not own them.

## Non-negotiable domain invariants

1. A student and an employee are separate profiles of one existing Person where applicable. Neither creates another human/authentication identity. A teacher who is a parent can have an individual staff principal and a distinct shared-family principal; grants, audit actors and data contexts never merge between them. Family actions are attributed to the shared principal, not a guessed adult.
2. Stable internal UUID, permanent school-unique Student-domain ID, permanent Admissions-allocated admission number, admission serial/sequence and roll number are distinct. Student ID and admission number are never reused and are allocated only at actual Student-creation/successful admission handoff. School Owner configures academic-context or persistent-student roll policy; its authoritative numeric component is separate from display, initial default starts at `10000` in a five-digit space, and authorized manual adjustment preserves history. All allocation is server-side, collision/concurrency-safe and idempotent; never `MAX()+1`. Age and current enrollment summaries are derived.
3. Student lifecycle and enrollment lifecycle are distinct. One Student has at most one normal active PRIMARY placement at a time; programs/electives use separate later assignments. A status transition retains previous/new state, effective date, reason, verified actor and recorded time. Enrollment placement in year/campus/class/section/roll remains reconstructable after promotion, repeat, class/section change, transfer, withdrawal, re-enrollment or graduation. New placement never overwrites the old authoritative placement.
4. No historical enrollment/status/assignment is hard-deleted through normal operations. Corrections preserve old fact, new fact, reason, actor, approval when required and audit. Academic-year/default-year changes and archive never hide authorized history or reuse an archived offering as current.
5. **APPROVED:** class-offering **and section** capacity are required for enrollment; either full limit normally blocks. A school-policy-permitted override needs explicit action permission, reason, verified actor, audit and concurrency-safe enforcement, but no approval workflow merely for the override. Role templates may recommend Owner/Principal/Administrator and omit Accountant; role name is never the permission check. D1B selects counted states and atomic mechanism; no read-then-write race.
6. **APPROVED:** one designated class teacher per section/effective time, with distinct section-specific Subject assignments. Different sections may have different teachers for one Subject; one teacher may span sections/classes/subjects/campuses. Subject assignment kinds are explicit PRIMARY (normally one active per Subject+section), CO_TEACHER and time-bounded SUBSTITUTE; accidental overlapping PRIMARY assignments are forbidden. Historical replacements remain. Employee active state and teacher capability interval must fit the assignment. Assignment is contextual evidence, not a grant by itself. `role + action + complete grant + matching scope + assignment + target state + workflow` must all pass. Visibility does not imply attendance, marks, export or correction authority; unknown resolver denies.
7. Private domain files reference Foundation `file_objects` through typed, owner-checked relationships. Server entitlement, enabled module, deployment-owned purpose, validated bytes and current domain authorization are separate gates. Existing private files remain after entitlement downgrade; neither object key nor signed URL is authority. Exact purpose activation remains TBD.
8. Foundation approval orchestrates only registered typed domain operations. Student/enrollment/assignment domains validate intent and apply results; no arbitrary JSON patch or direct authenticated DML. Sensitive profile corrections, status transitions, transfers and assignment corrections require reason/history/audit and their reviewed workflow policy. Capacity override specifically requires permission/reason/actor/audit, **not** approval solely for capacity. Audit and outbox are recorded with successful domain effect and command receipt where applicable.
9. Offline reads are encrypted and scoped; queued writes carry stable intent/idempotency and expected versions, then recheck current authority and current capacity/state on replay. Promotion, transfer, roll allocation, status, relationship/access and teacher assignment cannot use last-write-wins. Server remains authoritative. No full offline protocol is selected here.

## Student and enrollment operation semantics

| Operation | Student lifecycle | Enrollment/history effect |
|---|---|---|
| Initial enrollment | Requires a student created by an authorized admission or direct-school intake path; applicant alone is insufficient. | Create first placement with year, campus, offering/section and roll allocated by protected command. |
| Promotion | Student may remain Active subject to policy and finalized-result input from later Results. | Close/complete old placement; create a new-year placement linked to its source. A failed/repeat/manual outcome is an explicit decision, not a blind grade increment. |
| Repeat | Does not create a second student. | New placement in the appropriate year, potentially the same class definition, with its own roll and reason. |
| Class or section change | Usually leaves student lifecycle unchanged. | End old effective placement and start a new one, retaining the prior class/section. Validate both scopes and capacity. |
| Campus transfer | Internal movement does not automatically set the student to Transferred; that lifecycle state means departure to another institution. | End source-campus placement and create authorized destination placement, retaining history. |
| Withdrawal | Student status may become Withdrawn under approved transition policy. | End active placement, retain its year/campus/section/roll history. |
| Re-enrollment | Withdrawn/Transferred may return through an authorized process; Suspended may return to Active. | Create a new placement where appropriate; do not reopen or overwrite a historic one. |
| Graduation/expulsion/deceased | Normal reactivation from Graduated, Expelled or Deceased is barred; exceptional factual correction is privileged. Alumni classification preserves Graduation history. | No contradictory active placement may remain after effective terminal transition. |

Student status answers school relationship; enrollment status answers a particular placement's participation. Reports must count effective placements for rosters and effective student statuses for lifecycle statistics, not substitute one for the other. D1B designs exact temporal/transition enforcement against the approved D1-07/D1-08 rules.

Normal protected commands may update non-sensitive configuration/profile details with version checks. Exceptional capacity placement needs elevated action permission, reason, actor and audit but no approval solely for the override. Sensitive profile correction, status transition, transfer, enrollment correction, family-access change and retroactive teaching assignment correction need a reason, retained history and audit, with their configured approval requirement reviewed before activation. Domain validators, not Foundation's generic request payload, decide legality.

## Reporting, search and AI contract

Current roster and status summaries derive from effective history rather than replace it. Authorized historical queries specify year and campus context; broad search, exports, notifications and AI use the same row and field restrictions as direct reads. The approved ROSTER, PROFILE, RESTRICTED IDENTITY and SENSITIVE/SPECIAL levels require checked field-limited reads after student-row scope filtering; identity documents, private address/guardian contacts, disability/orphan indicators and blood group cannot leak through a roster. Employee private information and future medical/payroll facts remain restricted. No read model or index is approved here.

## Later package handoffs

Admissions hands off approved applicant intent and its allocated permanent admission number to idempotent Student creation/enrollment; Student separately allocates Student ID. Academic Operations binds curriculum to D1's stable Subject catalog and owns electives/assessments. Timetable consumes current teaching/room assignment and checks conflicts itself. Attendance, learning and results query historical enrollment/assignment context without rewriting it. HR/payroll consume employee identity and own compensation. Emergency Contact is a separate history-bearing student relationship with **no** portal grant; Employee Campus Affiliation is history-bearing operational context with **no** permission grant. Each handler must register typed permission, target, approval, audit and file-purpose contracts before activation.
