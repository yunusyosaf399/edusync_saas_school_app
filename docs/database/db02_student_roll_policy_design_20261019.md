## 1. Authority and existing implementation

Read `AGENTS.md`, master v0.2, decisions/invariants, SaaS/database guardrails, security/workflow/audit, readiness [#154](154_d1_student_roll_policy_change_p1_contract_readiness.md) and [#127](127_d1_roll_policy_p1_implementation_readiness_and_invariant_review.md), physical catalog [#27](27_domain_package_01_physical_catalog.md), constraints [#30](30_domain_package_01_constraint_matrix.md), lock graph [#31](31_domain_package_01_concurrency_and_locking.md), index ledger [#32](32_domain_package_01_index_review.md), retention [#34](34_domain_package_01_query_retention_offline_review.md), command [#09](../security/09_domain_checked_reads_and_commands.md), permissions [#08](../security/08_domain_permission_disclosure_catalog.md), and final pertinent non-executable roll/placement SQL fragments.

* School is the isolation/configuration target. A School can have multiple campuses, but there is no per-campus roll-policy authority or extra tenant ID. `roll_policy_revisions` is the HE authoritative history. Fields: `id`, `school_id`, `revision`, `mode`, `start_value`, `effective_from`, nullable `effective_until`, `reason`, nullable `supersedes_id`, and shared `created_at/created_by/ended_at/ended_by` evidence. **There is no HE `row_version` or `command_receipt_id`.**
* Modes: `PERSISTENT_STUDENT` and `ACADEMIC_CONTEXT`. Nonnegative numeric `start_value`, normally 10000 by default; the five-digit presentation is not a collision predicate. Dates use `[effective_from,effective_until)` (NULL means unbounded); end > start, strict one-way end, unchanged historical mode/start/reason, retained revisions and school+positive sequential revision key, exact earlier same-school predecessor; one school policy at a given date.
* `roll_allocator_states` is M-state keyed by `policy_revision_id` and nullable `class_offering_id`; `next_value >= 0`, server-managed row version; `PU01` enforces one NULL persistent pool per revision and `PU02` one non-NULL academic pool per policy+Class Offering. School/context ancestry is checked, not inferred from a NULL sentinel.
* `roll_allocations` is HE history with `enrollment_id`, `student_id`, `policy_revision_id`, `numeric_value`, `effective_from/until`, `origin_allocation_id`, `supersedes_id`, `correction_reason`. Numeric values are authoritative; display strings derive from policy/offering. Old allocation rows must never be renumbered, repointed, rewritten or deleted by a policy change.
* The committed current checked read `app.d1_read_roll_policy(uuid)` exposes only current policy id/mode/start/date under separate `student.roll_policy.view` authority; it is **not** a change, review or historical read.
* The registered operation `student.roll_policy.change` and its command permission are **disabled**; no accepted authenticated change, approval or apply RPC exists. Reviewer permission is `student.roll_policy.approve`. Both have non-family-safe **A = ALL/DIRECT** school authority, not CAMPUS/CLASS/SECTION. Suite #38 proves **owner-fixture structural history only**, and suite #65 pins the disabled operation/permission and direct DML denial. Do not interpret owner-fixture INSERT/UPDATE as a protected business command.
* Existing placement rolls are server-selected using policy effective at placement date: prelock `d1_enrollment_place_prelock_roll` locks the School via `d1_enrollment_place_lock_school`, then policy and exact mode-specific allocator; final `d1_enrollment_place_allocate_roll` selects current-as-of policy, reuses the latest unsuperseded same-Student persistent roll or reserves a counter, and checks academic collision by policy revision plus all Sections of a Class Offering. The later `effect35_15` definition lets the M-row trigger own allocator `row_version/updated_at` (counter update is `next_value` only). Existing move/end/correction logic is **owned elsewhere**.
* Indexed read/collision paths already proposed/installed: U08 (school,revision), I10 (school,effective_from DESC), PU01/PU02 allocator, I11 (class_offering_id), I12–I14 for allocation-by-enrollment/student/policy+numeric. New indexes are not authorized solely by this document.
* Serialization: Foundation `(71001,1)` **SHARED**, Foundation receipt/principal/workflow prefix, then School → policy revisions → allocator states → Student (when applicable) → academic path → child histories. Exact locks and reauthorization after waits. Foundation administration takes corresponding EXCLUSIVE lock. `schoolos_student_executor` is not schema owner; other executor RLS, receipt and audit separation remain.
* **Source inconsistency to resolve, not silently adopt:** proposed physical catalog #27 casually labels an audit event `student.roll_policy_changed`, but the later accepted command/event matrix #09 and readiness #127/#154 expressly leave the selected broad event **unspecified** (em dash) and prohibit inventing that event. The later review takes precedence; event name is **PENDING RP-06**.

### Namespace semantics already frozen

| Policy mode | Allocator key | Numeric collision/continuity domain | Accepted existing treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PERSISTENT_STUDENT` | `(policy_revision_id, NULL)` | A Student retains the same numeric component across later persistent placements/policy revisions, unless separately approved correction; another Student cannot claim the School-wide persistent lineage. | Check retained cross-revision school ownership; origin links earlier same-Student persistent allocation. Different policies must not reset ownership. |
| `ACADEMIC_CONTEXT` | `(policy_revision_id, class_offering_id)` | Exact policy revision + Class Offering, across all Sections of that Offering, at intersecting effective intervals. | Same number in a distinct policy-revision/Offering namespace may be valid. Section alone is **not** a namespace. |

**Important final-code review gate:** the current `effect35_15` persistent candidate loop excludes rows with a successor (`NOT EXISTS (... supersedes_id = r.id)`); compare this against the frozen **retained School-wide numeric ownership** rule before accepting policy handoff. A corrected-away historic number must not become available to a different Student merely because the original row is now superseded, unless the owner expressly chooses narrower ownership. This is a cross-domain DB-01/DB-03 compatibility risk, **not** permission for DB-02 to edit the allocator.

### Suggested typed business envelope (NOT a public function signature)

```text
Operation: student.roll_policy.change [contract_version existing; handler remains disabled]
school_id: uuid                              -- server-verified exact School
action: INIT | REPLACE | END                -- proposal only
source_policy_id: uuid?                     -- required REPLACE/END; absent INIT
expected_revision: integer?                 -- exact old revision, not mutable row_version
expected_source_mode: enum?                 -- snapshot predicate; REPLACE/END
expected_source_start_value: bigint?        -- snapshot predicate; REPLACE/END
expected_source_effective_from: date?       -- snapshot predicate; REPLACE/END
expected_source_effective_until: date?      -- snapshot predicate; NULL for open source
new_mode: enum?                             -- INIT/REPLACE only
new_start_value: bigint?                    -- INIT/REPLACE only
reason: nonblank private text
idempotency_key: validated opaque string
effective_date: server-derived CURRENT_DATE -- NOT caller supplied, if RP-02 approved
```

The server must verify School anchor, immutable source snapshot, monotonically assigned successor revision, current effective interval, allocator/placement dependencies, required current scope and route, and transaction-local immutable intent digest. The receipt identity and P1 request are Foundation-owned; no actor/role/permission/scope/route/client-supplied School-Campus list is trusted. School may be fixed from the single-school deployment and must match the stored source. Avoid introducing a public generic function or adding the proposed payload as broad arbitrary request JSON.

### Proposed state transitions and examples

* **INIT**: no history → revision 1 `PERSISTENT_STUDENT` at server date, counter provisioned. This does **not** create Students or admissions.
* **REPLACE**: `[2026-01-01,2026-10-19)` persistent r1 → `[2026-10-19,∞)` academic r2. An earlier enrolled Student's r1 allocation keeps its r1 ID and number; later placement at r2 uses its Class Offering pool, not a forced renumber of r1.
* **REPLACE back into persistent**: revision 3 has a *new* counter row, but existing retained school-wide persistent numeric owners remain reserved. A returning Student's existing persistent claim requires an explicitly frozen carry-forward rule; new Students must not collide.
* **END**: r2 closes at its exclusive boundary and no r3 exists. A placement whose effective date is in the resulting uncovered interval **must fail closed**, not fall back to r1 or invent numbers. If committed future placements exist, END should deny instead of quietly orphaning their policy coverage. END's product support remains pending.

## 3. Proposed exact physical/data integrity and query plan

**No new DB-02 base table is proposed.** Relations/links:
`school_profiles(id)` → `roll_policy_revisions(school_id)` (U08 revision identity; `supersedes_id` same-school predecessor), `roll_policy_revisions(id)` → `roll_allocator_states(policy_revision_id)` (PU01/PU02); `roll_policy_revisions(id)` → `roll_allocations(policy_revision_id)`. Academic allocator optional `class_offering_id` must reference an offering in that policy's school; roll allocation Enrollment→Section→Class Offering establishes contextual namespace. Allocator rows M-versioned/counter mutable; policy and allocation rows immutable HE except single permitted close.

The accepted policy has no existing policy-overlap exclusion guarantee purely through one column UNIQUE index: serialized School lock plus restricted defensive interval/supersession checks must enforce non-overlap, exactly-sequential revision, and immutable history. A replacement closes the correct prior open head once and inserts successor in **one transaction**; no FK/trigger may discover lower-rank parents after child locks. Do not use `MAX(numeric)+1` for allocation or let a new revision reset persistent ownership. Counter advances are server-trigger-versioned, compare supplied actual next-value where any, and check bigint overflow.

**Policy-time/placement-time mismatch gate:** audit final effective-date choice by `d1_enrollment_place_prelock_roll`, `d1_enrollment_place_allocate_roll`, `d1_move_student_enrollment` and `d1_end_student_enrollment`. A future-effective Enrollment committed under r1 must not become semantically orphaned by a later r2 covering its start. Proposed safe path: deny policy replacement or END when it would invalidate already committed future placement/roll without an explicitly approved coordinated migration. No silent rewrite/retroactive reallocation.

**RLS/permissions:** all three relations remain `app_private` with ENABLE+FORCE RLS; no client direct SELECT/DML. Reuse `schoolos_authz_reader` fixed eval, `schoolos_student_executor` minimum policy/allocator changes, `schoolos_workflow_executor` Foundation P1 stages, `schoolos_evidence_writer` fixed evidence, and `schoolos_read_executor` bounded current/participant reads. Any needed school-anchor lock privilege must be provided by an existing fixed helper rather than opening generic School DML or owner membership. No new roles, seeded grants, arbitrary JSON PATCH, or future Admissions integration.

**Temporal display:** existing allocation continues pointing at its immutable policy revision even after replacement; historical display must resolve that retained revision and original academic context, not join to today's School policy. The current checked policy read still returns only the currently effective policy and must never imply historical policy access.

## 4. DB-01 and DB-03 dependency contract (no cross-domain edits)

| Contract/decision to export | DB-01 — `student.roll.correct` | DB-03 — enrollment placement/move/end and roll issuance |
|---|---|---|
| Effective policy at date | Correction binds the *existing allocation's immutable* policy revision; policy replacement cannot retroactively change it. | Placement/move must select exactly one policy covering operation-effective date under School anchor, even at future boundaries. |
| Namespace | Persistent cross-revision Student ownership, academic exact revision+Offering; no override from client. | Provision exact pool before placing; School→policy→allocator locking before Student/academic locks. |
| Corrections and counter | DB-01's manual high-water/collision and corrected-away retained number reuse proposal needs owner sign-off; no DB-02 implementation. | Auto allocation must not reuse another Student's persistent historical numeric claim; check final allocator scan and MOVE/END ordering. |
| New policy cutover | Retain correction origin/supersedes, even if later School policy changes; no bulk re-number. | Existing committed future placements need a binding gate; reject conflicting policy edits until product decision resolves it. |
| Concurrency | Cross-command policy/roll-correction race must agree on School anchor and stable lock prefix. | Cross-command policy/placement/move/end must use same prefix and recheck current policy and serializable choice under observed locks. |
| Approval/replay | DB-01 RC-01…RC-08 remain independently unapproved; do not inherit RP approval as RC approval. | No silent automatic enrollment or roll allocation if a School has no policy after END; Admissions remains a separate prerequisite. |
