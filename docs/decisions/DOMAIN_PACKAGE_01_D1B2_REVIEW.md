## Product decisions and boundaries

There are **zero product-owner questions required to complete D1B2**. No complete Student/Employee state-transition edge matrix is invented: D1B3 may expose only the explicitly approved transitions and must obtain a separate product decision before activating another edge. No single-primary-job restriction or requirement that SUBSTITUTE overlap an existing PRIMARY is fabricated. A substitute may be represented without that structural dependence, but operational permission still requires the complete grant and effective assignment. Later Admissions, curriculum, HR compensation/contracts/leave, attendance, medical, worker and UI packages remain deferred.
